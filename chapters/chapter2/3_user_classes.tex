\section{User Classes and Characteristics}

The system includes multiple user roles, each with specific responsibilities. All users are expected to have basic knowledge of computers and web browsers.

\noindent Administrator:
Manages the entire system, including user permissions and system security. This role oversees the application process and manages essential operational tasks such as enforcing deadlines.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Full system control.
    \item Manage users and roles.
    \item Control admission deadlines.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Deans' Office:
Charged with verifying education documents and assessing the eligibility of applicants. The office approves or rejects applications based on specific faculty requirements and demands.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Verify documents.
    \item Approve or reject applications.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Hall Office:
Responsible for managing financial clearance and ensuring that all residency-related documentation and requirements are satisfied for the students.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Verify payment and clearance.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Medical Office:
Oversees the health evaluation process and manages the generation of medical reports to ensure that every applicant meets the necessary medical requirements for admission.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Generate medical reports.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Registrar's Office:
Acts as the final authority to finalize admission decisions once all prior verification processes are complete. This office is also responsible for maintaining and holding student records.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Final admission approval.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Applicants:
The primary users who facilitate the submission of application forms and verify their own necessary documents through the portal. They use the system to track their application progress in real-time.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Submit application.
    \item Upload documents.
    \item Track status.
\end{itemize}
