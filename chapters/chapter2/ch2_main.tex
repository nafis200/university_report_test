\section{Chapter Overview}

In this chapter, the system requirement will be looked into, both functional and non-functional. First, the main aim of this system is to automate the university admission processes using information technology. For an efficient automation of university admission process, the stakeholders are grouped into different categories for their different tasks and they include Admin, Dean, Hall, Medical and Registrar, alongside with Applicants. In terms of its technical architecture, it is characterized by the interfaces with the system, including the graphical interface, database interface and authentication. Functionally, the system is modularized where each module carries out particular task for example Dean verifies applicants; Medical and Hall offices grant clearance while Applicant module is used for creating and submitting profile of the students.This section ends with the non-functional requirements which must be in place to ensure the stability of the system by ensuring high performance and scalability so as to accommodate the heavy flow of traffic especially during times when admissions are open.
