\section{Dean Module: Data Redundancy and Sync}

\noindent To keep student records safe, the Dean Module has a feature that automatically syncs data.

\subsection{Google Drive Integration}

\noindent The system lets the Dean sync the list of applicants who have been verified to a Google Drive account reserved for faculty members.
