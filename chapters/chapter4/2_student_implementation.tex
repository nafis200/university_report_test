\section{Student Module Implementation}

\noindent When students log in successfully, they are directed to their application portal. The main task for a student is to complete the admission form by providing accurate personal and academic information.

\subsection{Final Admission Form}

\noindent The admission form is designed to collect detailed information about the student's home and identity. Each field is validated before submission to ensure the accuracy of the data.

\setlength{\fboxsep}{6pt}
\setlength{\fboxrule}{1pt}

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \fbox{\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{chapters/chapter4/images/form.png}}
    \caption{Student Admission Form with Validation}
    \label{fig:student_form}
\end{figure}

\noindent As shown in Figure \ref{fig:student_form}, the form contains the following sections:

\begin{itemize}
    \item {Address Details:} Students must provide their \textit{Village/House number}, \textit{Post Office}, \textit{Thana}, \textit{District}, and \textit{Country}. Mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (*).
    
    \item {Postal Code Validation:} The {Post Code} field requires exactly 4 digits, preventing incorrect entries.

    \item {Identification Information:} Students can provide either their {NID}, {Birth Registration}, or {Passport number}.

    \item {Real-time Error Handling:} The system immediately notifies the student of errors. If a field is empty or contains invalid data, a red error message appears to guide correction.

    \item {Bilingual Interface:} All labels are shown in both Bengali and English, ensuring all students can understand and complete the form accurately.
\end{itemize}

\noindent This structured approach ensures the database receives validated and formatted information, which simplifies verification by the Dean and Registrar offices. The admission form is the key component of the Student Module, which is essential for a smooth admission process.
