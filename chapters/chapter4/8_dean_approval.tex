\section{Dean Module: Student Status Management}
The Dean holds the authority to either approve a student for a department or cancel the application based on academic discrepancies.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.85\textwidth]{chapters/chapter4/images/approvedList.png}
    \caption{Dean's Portal: Admission Approval and Cancellation Interface}
    \label{fig:dean_status_manage}
\end{figure}

The Dean can perform the following actions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Approve Admission:} If the student's marks and documents are valid, the Dean clicks the "Approve" button to move the applicant to the next stage.
    \item \textbf{Cancel Admission:} In case of fraudulent data or failing to meet departmental criteria, the Dean has the provision to cancel the admission status immediately.
\end{itemize}
