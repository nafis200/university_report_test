\subsection{Submission Confirmation and Complaint Management}

\noindent When a student submits their application form they see a confirmation message. This message tells them that their information is safe in our database.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{chapters/chapter4/images/complain.png}
    \caption{Form Submission Confirmation and Complaint Interface}
    \label{fig:submission_status}
\end{figure}

\noindent The confirmation page has some features:

\begin{itemize}
    \item {Success Notification:} A green alert box pops up. Says their information is saved. This gives students peace of mind.

    \item {PDF. Download:} Students can save their application form as a PDF. This way they can look at it again later. This feature is part of our report generation system.

    \item {Complaint Management:} If students find an error in their data or have issues they can report it away.

    \item {Action Buttons:} There are buttons like ``Download PDF'' and ``Report a Problem''. These buttons help students know what to do.
\end{itemize}

\noindent This feature makes our system more reliable. It also helps students away and makes sure they have a good experience with the Digital Admission Procedure Management for Post-GST Candidates. The complaint management helps students report any issues with their application form. The system provides an experience, for students.
