\section{Module of Medical Officer: Synchronization of Data}

For the safekeeping of the student's health information in the future, the feature of cloud synchronization has been added to the Medical Module.

\subsection{Cloud Storage for Health Information}

The Medical Officer will be able to synchronize all health-related data and clearing information on the Google Drive of the university.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{chapters/chapter4/images/uploadDrive.png}
    \caption{Synchronization of Health Data and Google Drive}
    \label{fig:medical_drive_sync}
\end{figure}

Through this process, the university medical department would have a perpetual and secure digital copy of all the enrolled students.
