\subsubsection{Show Applicant Profile}
This is where the Hall Office gets an opportunity to view the profile of the applicant, but after searching for him or her. The profile will contain all details about the applicant, ranging from personal information to his/her academic details and the status of the application. Access to the profile of the applicant becomes easier with just a click on the "Profile" button.
