\subsubsection{Download Application Form}

This use case provides the applicant with the ability to generate and download a formal PDF version of their submitted application. It serves as an official record for the applicant, which may be required during the physical verification process at \textit{Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST)}.

    \centering
    \caption{Use Case Table: Download Application Form (UC-1.7)}
    \label{tab:use_case_download_form}
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
        \hline
        \rowcolor[gray]{0.9} \textbf{Characteristics} & \textbf{Description} \\ \hline

        Use Case Name & Download Application Form \\ \hline
        Primary Actors & Applicant \\ \hline
        Goal in Context & To obtain a digital or physical copy of the completed admission form. \\ \hline
        Precondition & The applicant must be signed in and must have successfully submitted the application form. \\ \hline
        Post-condition & A PDF file is generated and successfully downloaded to the user's local storage. \\ \hline
        Triggers & The applicant clicks the \textbf{Download} or \textbf{Print Application} button on their dashboard. \\ \hline
        Scenario & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item The applicant navigates to their personal dashboard.
            \item The system checks the application status to ensure it has been submitted.
            \item The applicant clicks the \textbf{Download PDF} button.
            \item The Next.js server-side logic generates a PDF document containing the applicant's data from the database.
            \item The browser initiates a file download for the generated PDF.
            \item The applicant saves the file to their device for future reference.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Exception & \textbf{Generation Error:} If the server fails to fetch data or the PDF library encounters an error, an "Internal Server Error" message is displayed. \\ \hline
        Frequency of Use & High (Typically once per applicant after submission). \\ \hline
