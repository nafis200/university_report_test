\subsubsection{View Applicant List}

This use case describes the procedure for administrative actors to access and review the records of candidates who have applied for admission. It allows various administrative offices (Dean, Hall, Medical, Registrar) to filter and monitor the status of applicants throughout the admission lifecycle.

    \centering
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}

        \rowcolor[gray]{0.9} \textbf{Characteristics} & \textbf{Description} \\ \hline
        Use Case ID & UC-1.2 \\ \hline
        Use Case Name & View Applicant List \\ \hline
        Primary Actors & Admin, Dean Office, Hall Office, Medical Office, Registrar Office \\ \hline
        Goal in Context & To filter and view different applicant categories (All, Admitted, or Waiting). \\ \hline
        Precondition & The user must be securely authenticated and signed into the administrative dashboard. \\ \hline
        Post-condition & The requested list of applicants is fetched and displayed on the UI. \\ \hline
        Triggers & Need to verify candidate status or proceed with departmental clearance. \\ \hline
        Scenario & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item The user visits the Dashboard after a successful login.
            \item The user clicks on the 'Applicant Management' or 'View List' option from the sidebar.
            \item The user chooses a specific list category (e.g., All Applicants, Admitted, or Waiting).
            \item The Next.js backend fetches the data from the PostgreSQL database through Prisma.
            \item The system displays the applicant records in a responsive data table.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Exception & \textbf{Access Denied:} If the user’s role does not have permission to view certain lists, the system shows an unauthorized access message. \\ \hline
        Frequency of Use & High (Used frequently for administrative tasks). \\ \hline
