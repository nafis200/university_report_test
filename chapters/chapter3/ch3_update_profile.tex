\subsubsection{Update Profile}

This use case allows registered applicants to manage and modify their personal profile information within the \textit{JUST Undergraduate Final Admission System}. It ensures that applicants can keep their contact details and personal records up to date throughout the admission process.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
    \centering
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
        \hline
        \rowcolor[gray]{0.9} \textbf{Characteristics} & \textbf{Description} \\ \hline
        Use Case ID & UC-1.8 \\ \hline
        Use Case Name & Update Profile \\ \hline
        Primary Actors & Applicant \\ \hline
        Goal in Context & To successfully update and save the applicant's personal profile information. \\ \hline
        Precondition & The applicant must be securely signed into the system. \\ \hline
        Post-condition & The updated profile details are saved in the PostgreSQL database and reflected on the UI. \\ \hline
        Triggers & The applicant needs to change or correct their personal details, such as phone number or address. \\ \hline
        Scenario & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item The applicant navigates to the \textbf{Profile} or \textbf{Settings} section from the dashboard.
            \item The system displays the current profile information in an editable form.
            \item The applicant modifies the necessary information fields.
            \item The applicant clicks the \textbf{Save Changes} or \textbf{Update Profile} button.
            \item The system validates the input and updates the record via the Prisma ORM.
            \item A success message is displayed, confirming the profile update.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Exception & \textbf{Session Timeout:} If the user is inactive for too long, the system redirects them to the login page without saving changes. \\ \hline
        Frequency of Use & Medium to High (As needed by the applicant). \\ \hline
    \end{tabularx}
