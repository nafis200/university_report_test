\subsubsection{Pending Documents and Reminders}

This section covers receiving pending documents and sending reminder messages to applicants who have yet to complete their documentation.

        Primary Actors & Dean Office \\ \hline
        Scenario (Reminder) & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item Identify applicant with missing documents.
            \item Select the missing items and click \textbf{Send Message}.
            \item System triggers a notification to the applicant's contact.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Scenario (Receive) & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item Click \textbf{Pending Documents Action} from the sidebar.
            \item Update the record upon receiving physical copies.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
