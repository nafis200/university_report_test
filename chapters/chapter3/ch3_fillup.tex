\subsubsection{Fill-Up Application Form}

This use case will describe how the process of filling out the application form using the \textit{JUST Undergraduate Final Admission System} works. This process involves several steps in order to obtain the documentation and other important information needed for the final admission validation.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
    \centering
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|l|X|}
        \hline
        \rowcolor[gray]{0.9} \textbf{Characteristics} & \textbf{Description} \\ \hline
        Use Case ID & UC-1.6 \\ \hline
        Use Case Name & Fill-Up Application Form \\ \hline
        Primary Actors & Applicant \\ \hline
        Goal in Context & To successfully submit the completed admission application form. \\ \hline
        Precondition & The user needs to sign in and should not have a pending application already submitted. \\ \hline
        Post-condition & The admission application form is saved in the database under the "Pending" status. \\ \hline
        Triggers & Clicking "Apply now" or "Fill-up Form" from the dashboard menu. \\ \hline
        Scenario & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item The applicant goes to the admission form section.
            \item The applicant sees that the form consists of several stages (Personal Information, Academic History, Document Upload).
            \item The applicant fills in the form with the needed data (Photo, Signature, etc.).
            \item The applicant checks if the entered data is correct.
            \item The applicant clicks the Submit button.
            \item The Next.js frontend passes the information to the API, then it is saved to PostgreSQL via Prisma.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Exception & \textbf{Validation Error:} If required fields are missing or uploaded files are in unsupported formats, the system prompts the applicant to correct the errors. \\ \hline
        Frequency of Use & Once per applicant (High during admission periods). \\ \hline
    \end{tabularx}
