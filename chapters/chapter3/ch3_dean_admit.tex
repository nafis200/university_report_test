\subsubsection{Admit Applicant (Dean Clearance)}

This use case allows the Dean Office to grant final clearance for admission after successful verification of all records.

        Goal in Context & To grant final admission clearance to the applicant. \\ \hline
        Precondition & All documents must be verified and the applicant must be eligible. \\ \hline
        Post-condition & Applicant status is changed to 'Admitted' and department seat count is updated. \\ \hline
        Scenario & 
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep, leftmargin=*]
            \item Search for an eligible applicant record.
            \item Review the final summary of the candidate.
            \item Click the \textbf{Admit} button.
            \item System updates the record in PostgreSQL.
        \end{enumerate} \\ \hline
        Exception & Seat capacity full or role unauthorized. \\ \hline
        Frequency of Use & High \\ \hline
