\subsubsection{Seat Status and Export}

In this particular use case scenario, the objective revolves around monitoring seat availability and exporting application information for administration.
The Dean’s office would be able to check in real time how many seats are available or occupied. 
This will enable them to make informed judgments concerning admission acceptance and rejections. 
The application also offers an export function that generates lists of applicants in either CSV or Excel formats. 
Users are allowed to filter the applicants according to their requirements before exporting them. 
This function is designed to enhance transparency in seat availability and admission tracking. 
Furthermore, it minimizes the amount of manual work required to extract data from the system.
